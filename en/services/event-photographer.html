Corporate Event and Show Photographer

Because each event is unique and requires various photo services, I offer a wide range of options that you can combine at a tailor-made price. There is the photo report on DVD delivered a few days after the event. It consists of a number of pictures of the event that show the essentials:

  • Pictures taken on the spot
  • Pictures of reactions
  • Pictures of the atmosphere
  • Official pictures
  • Group pictures
  • Pictures of the setting
  • Etc.

Here are other possible options:

  • Picture display on giant screens
  • Fast printing on location (portraits or pictures of people at the tables) or after the event
  • Choice of customized picture frames
  • Picture printing according to theme (Photoshop editing)
  • Musical photomontage (with printed DVD copies)
  • Etc.

    • As you can see, there are several options available! Do not hesitate to make special requests.
