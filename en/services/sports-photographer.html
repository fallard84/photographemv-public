Sports Photographer

I offer you my services to take action or official photos for any sport. I can deliver the photographs on DVD, print and even frame them on location or on the days following the event. You can choose any kind of picture frame, including tailor-made frames, laminated pictures or reproductions on canvas. It is also possible to create a set-up with logos and titles, like official photos of sports teams during a tournament or the name of the team and the date of the event for the beginning of the season.

Golf Tournaments

As for golf tournaments, I offer various packages according to the number of participants and the service you choose. It can be photo reports on the field (can be humoristic), delivered on DVD and/or shown on giant screens during the evening; official team or individual photographs, delivered on DVD or printed and framed on location (as a gift to the participants or to raise money for a charitable organization). I offer many conventional frame options and I can create customized frames for your event. It is possible to obtain other services depending on the special requests, do not hesitate to ask. The packages can be adapted to your specific needs.
