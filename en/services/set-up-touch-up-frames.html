Set-up, Touch up and Frames

It is possible to create themed collages with pictures according to specific requests. These conceptual photographs can be printed or delivered on DVD. I can also add elements on the photos like logos or commemorative words (for team pictures, greeting cards, etc.)

You can send me photos that need touch up or restoration. I work on the pictures and improve them. Here are a few examples. You can click on the picture to see the original image.

I offer a wide range of picture frames for all occasions. It can be a stiff frame, made of Plexiglas, simple paperboard (several color choices) or even booklets. For the paperboard frames, it is possible to print (hot stamping - choice of different colors) a logo or commemorative words. I prepare the set-up and send it for approval before printing.

I also offer customized graphic design for frames (color printed frames). I can suggest you a style or you can submit me an idea or a theme. Original frames help make one-of-a-kind events!
